# DealerConnect AI

A modern, mobile-first, and premium local dealer marketplace connecting customers with nearby verified shops, real-time product catalogs, live price comparisons, and direct dealer inquiries.

## Features
- **Mobile-First & App Experience**: Custom app header, bottom tab navigation (Home, Categories, Compare, Cart, Profile), drawer hamburger menu, and draggable floating chatbot.
- **Dynamic Themes**: Multiple premium colour themes (Gold & Black, Green & White, Black & White, Corporate Blue, Royal Purple, Sunset Amber) dynamically configurable by the Admin.
- **Admin Command Center**: Complete control over application branding (name, logo, icon, colors), dynamic homepage layout, banners, featured sections, navigation, and push notifications.
- **Firebase Database & Authentication**: Robust real-time backend powered by Firebase Firestore and Firebase Authentication with resilient offline fallback.
- **Push Notifications**: Multi-segment push messaging (all customers, all dealers, or targeted users).
- **Profile & Security**: Comprehensive profile editing and secure password management for customers, dealers, and admins.
- **PWA & Android Ready**: Manifest V3 compliant, responsive standalone display, service worker, and Android TWA asset linking.
- **SEO & Social Optimization**: Dynamic OpenGraph, Twitter Cards, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, and schema.org JSON-LD microdata.
